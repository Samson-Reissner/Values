Security Best Practices

  • Never share your password, PIN, or 2FA codes with anyone
  • Use a unique password for your Value Wallet that you don't use elsewhere
  • Enable 2-factor authentication for maximum security
  • Log out from shared or public computers immediately after use
  • Regularly review your active sessions and end any you don't recognize
  • Keep your recovery keys in a safe place separate from your devices
  • Be cautious of phishing emails or messages asking for your credentials
  • Keep your devices and apps updated with the latest security patches
